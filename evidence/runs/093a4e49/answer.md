# Write a haiku about failing over, then explain the poem

**Haiku**

A service goes down,  
traffic reroutes in the dark—  
one system breathes on.

**Explanation**

The poem captures the moment a primary server or service fails and the load is shifted to a standby system. “A service goes down” states the failure plainly. “Traffic reroutes in the dark” conjures the automatic, unseen process of failover — requests are silently redirected without human intervention. The final line, “one system breathes on,” personifies the backup system as it quietly takes over, keeping operations alive even as the original has stopped.
