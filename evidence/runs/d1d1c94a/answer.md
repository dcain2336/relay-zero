# Name three constellations visible in October skies, with one line each on how to find them.

1. **Orion**: Look for the three bright stars in a row forming his "belt," then extend the line to find the bright star Betelgeuse to the east.
2. **Scorpius**: Search for the red supergiant Antares, which marks the heart of the scorpion, with a distinct curved tail hooking below it.
3. **Pegasus**: Identify the large square shape formed by four bright stars (the "Great Square"), which serves as the body of the horse.
